India

India is a vibrant South Asian country known for its rich history, diverse cultures, beautiful landscapes, and spiritual traditions. From the Himalayas to the Indian Ocean, India offers many unforgettable travel experiences.

1. Agra

Agra is home to the Taj Mahal, a magnificent white marble mausoleum built by Emperor Shah Jahan. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a famous symbol of love.

2. New Delhi

New Delhi, India's capital, features important landmarks such as India Gate, Humayun's Tomb, Qutub Minar, and the Red Fort.

3. Jaipur, Rajasthan

Known as the Pink City, Jaipur is famous for its royal architecture, Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, and colourful traditional markets.

4. Goa

Goa is known for its sandy beaches, Portuguese-era architecture, historic churches, water sports, and beautiful coastal scenery.

5. Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu is famous for Dravidian temples, including the Meenakshi Amman Temple in Madurai, the scenic hill station of Ooty, and the historic temples of Thanjavur.

United States of America (USA)

The United States is a diverse country in North America, famous for its iconic cities, entertainment, historical landmarks, and spectacular natural wonders.

1. New York City

New York City is famous for the Statue of Liberty, Times Square, Central Park, Broadway theatres, and the Empire State Building.

2. Grand Canyon National Park

Located in Arizona, the Grand Canyon is a spectacular natural wonder known for its immense cliffs, colourful rock layers, hiking trails, and panoramic views.

3. Las Vegas

Las Vegas, in Nevada, is renowned for its entertainment, luxury hotels, spectacular shows, and the famous Strip.

4. San Francisco

San Francisco is known for the Golden Gate Bridge, historic cable cars, Alcatraz Island, and its beautiful waterfront.

5. Yellowstone National Park

Yellowstone is famous for its geysers, hot springs, dramatic canyons, and wildlife. It became the world's first national park in 1872.

Russia

Russia is the world's largest country by land area, spanning Eastern Europe and Northern Asia. It offers historic palaces, famous museums, remarkable architecture, and vast natural landscapes.

1. Red Square and the Kremlin

Located in Moscow, Red Square and the Kremlin are among Russia's most famous landmarks. Visitors can admire historic buildings and the colourful domes of Saint Basil's Cathedral.

2. State Hermitage Museum

Situated in Saint Petersburg, the Hermitage Museum is one of the world's major art museums. It contains extensive art collections and occupies historic buildings, including the Winter Palace.

3. Lake Baikal

Located in Siberia, Lake Baikal is the world's deepest freshwater lake. It is known for its clear waters, unique wildlife, winter ice formations, and scenic surroundings.

4. Peterhof Palace

Peterhof Palace near Saint Petersburg is famous for its grand architecture, beautiful gardens, and impressive fountains. It is often called the Russian Versailles.

5. Trans-Siberian Railway

The Trans-Siberian Railway is a famous railway route crossing vast parts of Russia. Travellers can experience forests, mountains, cities, and the enormous Siberian landscape.
